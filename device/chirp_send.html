eHawk stand-in: chirp a signed directive

This page plays the role of the household eHawk for the demo. Step 1 simulates the NOAA SAME burst arriving (POST /alerts/same). The server signs a 16-byte directive. Step 2 plays it as sound. A phone with the EchoHawk app open, mic permission granted, in airplane mode, within arm's reach, decodes it, verifies the signature, and re-voices it in the household language. Audible mode is for the stage; it does not work in loud rooms, and the range is about one meter. That is the honest claim.
